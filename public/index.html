WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Scoring pace ≠ a forecast.

The pace projection assumes the current scoring rate continues. The historical panel checks how often previous WBBL teams reached that total from the same score and ball count.

Player strength, weather and bowling resources are not yet modelled. No betting signals are enabled.

TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

A frequency you can inspect.

Each result matches the exact team runs and legal balls elapsed. By default, it also matches the exact wicket count. The optional broader sample pools wicket counts explicitly; it never silently widens the score range.

Example

70 at 10 overs projects to 140. Across all wicket counts, 12 of 15 historical innings finished on 140 or more. Restricting to 70/2 gives 5 of 7. These small, different samples do not justify treating either percentage as a precise live probability.

What is included?

Completed, standard WBBL first innings from the downloaded 519-match Cricsheet archive. 463 innings meet the eligibility rules. Shortened, adjusted and incomplete innings, plus exceptional scoring/availability cases, are excluded.

What is still missing?

Player, venue, weather and opponent adjustments; target-aware chase modelling; prospective evaluation; verified individual live deliveries; market-price comparison. The score provider adapter is for snapshots and must be tested against actual provider responses before being relied on.

No certainty from a perfect sample

Four successes in four innings is an observed 100%, not a promise. Sample size and the displayed approximate 95% interval help reveal uncertainty. They do not correct for changes in teams, conditions or eras.

BUILD IT. UNDERSTAND IT.

Your first real app.

  1. The page

    Open public/index.html. Find the heading “What happened before?” and change it. Save, then refresh your browser.

  2. The design

    Open public/styles.css. The variables at the top control colours. Change --accent to try another highlight.

  3. The calculations

    Open src/cricket.mjs. oversToBalls converts 15.3 into 93 balls. paceProjection calculates the equivalent 20-over total.

  4. The automatic updates

    server.mjs sends updates to public/app.js. One server connection feeds every visitor; each visitor does not spend a separate provider request.

  5. The live source

    Copy .env.example to .env. Configure a provider key and match ID on your computer, then restart the server. Never put secrets into GitHub or the page.

  6. Your GitHub history

    Save each small working change as a commit. The README explains how to create and connect a private GitHub repository.
